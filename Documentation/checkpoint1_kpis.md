Business Question:
“How effective are the marketing campaigns, and how has their performance changed over time?”

At this stage, four key performance indicators (KPIs/metrics) were defined to evaluate the effectiveness of marketing campaigns and identify changes in their performance over time. The selected metrics are based on the available fields in the marketing_events dataset, including sessions, conversions, spend, and revenue.

1. Conversion Rate — Conversions / Sessions × 100
Measures the percentage of sessions that resulted in a conversion. This KPI is relevant for evaluating how effectively marketing traffic is being converted into successful outcomes.

2. Revenue — Total Revenue Generated
Measures the total revenue generated through marketing activities. It is relevant for evaluating the financial outcome and overall business value generated by the marketing campaigns.

3. ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) — Revenue / Spend
Measures the revenue generated for each unit of marketing spend. This KPI is relevant for evaluating the efficiency and return of marketing investments.

4. Conversions — Total Number of Conversions
Measures the total number of successful conversions generated by the marketing campaigns. It is relevant for evaluating the volume of successful outcomes and identifying changes in conversion performance over time.

Together, these KPIs provide a comprehensive view of marketing performance by measuring traffic effectiveness, revenue generation, marketing spend efficiency, and the volume of successful conversions. They will also support comparisons across different time periods in the following stages of the analysis.
