Specific Actionable Recommendation

Reallocate 20% of the current Paid Social marketing budget to Email campaigns for the next campaign period. This recommendation is based on the substantial ROAS difference between the two channels: Email generates approximately 128.2 ROAS, while Paid Social generates approximately 4.19 ROAS. Paid Social also has a significantly higher CPA, averaging approximately $10.87 per conversion, compared with approximately $0.44 for Email.

The reallocated budget should be used to expand high-performing Email campaigns and tested against the existing allocation. After the next campaign period, ROAS, conversion rate, and CPA should be compared with the previous period to determine whether the budget shift should be maintained or increased.
